About me

Hello, I am RTD, an author and artist passionate about art and animation.

Since I was little, I have liked to draw. I dedicate myself to creating all kinds of stories and drawing from them.

My style is characterized by drawing traditionally, then transferring it to digital. I am still trying to find a good style in digital, but in traditional I like to draw between realistic and animated, more animated than realistic.

When I am not creating, I like to listen to music and sleep.

If you want to contact me or follow my work, you can find me on Instagram, Twitter, I am with the same name, but soon I want to add buttons so you can go directly there. I am new to programming, to create this site I put together several pieces as best as I could, I am still studying and I am enjoying it although sometimes it is frustrating.

Sorry Sorry if I mostly speak in Spanish, I am not good at English.
